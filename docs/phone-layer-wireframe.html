Reading Notes

Normal flow

Header, persistent search, and scroll content are flex children inside .phone-rect. The search bar is not sticky; it stays visible because the scroll viewport is the flex child below it.

Compose

The search bar and suggestions become one .search-panel. Background content remains in the scroll area; results are dimmed when compose sits over results.

Selection

SelectionTray is absolute at z35. The viewport gets extra bottom padding so selected media does not disappear behind the tray.

Detail and sheets

Detail covers the whole phone at z50, above the black backdrop at z49. Bottom sheets are portaled in the app, but represented here as phone overlays at z101 or z201.
